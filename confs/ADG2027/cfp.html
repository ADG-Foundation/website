Call for Extended Abstracts

ADG 2027

Sixteenth International Conference on Automated Deduction in Geometry (including topics related to AI and geometry)

13–16 June 2027 — Université Paris Cité, Paris, and AMSS, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing

Overview

ADG is a forum to exchange ideas and views, to present research results and progress, and to demonstrate software tools at the intersection of geometry and AI (both symbolic AI and generative AI).

The conference is held every two years. The previous editions of ADG were held in Stuttgart in 2025, Belgrade in 2023, Hagenberg in 2021 (online, postponed from 2020 due to COVID-19), Nanning in 2018, Strasbourg in 2016, Coimbra in 2014, Edinburgh in 2012, Munich in 2010, Shanghai in 2008, Pontevedra in 2006, Gainesville in 2004, Hagenberg in 2002, Zurich in 2000, Beijing in 1998, and Toulouse in 1996.

The 16th edition, ADG 2027, will be held on 13–16 June 2027, jointly in Beijing and Paris.

Scope

Relevant topics include (but are not limited to):
  • AI-assisted geometric reasoning, formalization, automated deduction, synthesis;
  • applications of ADG in mechanics, geometric modeling, CAGD/CAD, computer vision, security, robotics, and education;
  • polynomial algebra, invariant and coordinate-free methods;
  • probabilistic, synthetic, and logical approaches, techniques for automated geometric reasoning from discrete mathematics, combinatorics, and numerics;
  • interactive theorem proving in geometry;
  • symbolic and numeric methods for geometric computation, geometric constraint solving, automated generation and manipulation of, and reasoning with, diagrams;
  • design and implementation of geometry software, automated theorem provers, special-purpose tools, experimental studies;
  • automated deduction in non-Euclidean geometries.

Submission Guidelines

We invite submissions of extended abstracts of 3 to 12 pages, excluding references.

Extended abstracts must be submitted in PDF format via HotCRP (link to follow).

Refereeing and Publication

The submitted extended abstracts will be reviewed by the Programme Committee.

Informal proceedings containing the accepted abstracts will be made available on the conference website. Authors remain free to submit their work to other venues.

The authors of extended abstracts accepted for presentation at the conference will be invited to submit their full papers for publication in the formal post-proceedings of ADG 2027 after the conference. A special issue of the journal Annals of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence (AMAI) was organized for ADG 2021, 2023, and 2025; earlier post-proceedings appeared as volumes of Lecture Notes in Artificial Intelligence (LNAI).
