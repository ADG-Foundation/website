Call for Extended Abstracts

ADG 2027

Sixteenth International Conference on Automated Deduction in Geometry (and topics related to AI and geometry)

13-16 June 2027, Paris, Université Paris Cité and Beijing, AMSS Chinese Academy of Sciences

Overview

ADG is a forum to exchange ideas and views, to present research results and progress, and to demonstrate software tools at the intersection between geometry and AI (both symbolic AI and generative AI).

The conference is held every two years. The previous editions of ADG were held in Stuttgart 2025, Belgrade in 2023, Hagenberg in 2021 (online, postponed from 2020 due to COVID-19), Nanning in 2018, Strasbourg in 2016, Coimbra in 2014, Edinburgh in 2012, Munich in 2010, Shanghai in 2008, Pontevedra in 2006, Gainesville in 2004, Hagenberg in 2002, Zürich in 2000, Beijing in 1998, and Toulouse in 1996.

The 16th edition, ADG 2027, will be held, 13-16 June 2027, bi-located in Beijing and Paris.

Scope

Relevant topics include (but are not limited to):
  • AI-assisted geometric reasoning, formalization, automated deduction, synthesis;
  • applications of ADG in mechanics, geometric modeling, CAGD/CAD, computer vision, security, robotics and education;
  • polynomial algebra, invariant and coordinate-free methods;
  • probabilistic, synthetic, and logic approaches, techniques for automated geometric reasoning from discrete mathematics, combinatorics, and numeric;
  • interactive theorem proving in geometry;
  • symbolic and numeric methods for geometric computation, geometric constraint solving, automated generation/reasoning and manipulation with diagrams;
  • design and implementation of geometry software, automated theorem provers, special-purpose tools, experimental studies;
  • automated deduction in non-euclidean geometries.

Submission Guidelines

We invite submission of extended abstracts (3 to 12 pages excluding bibliography).

Extended abstracts are to be submitted as PDF via EasyChair (TODO).

Refereeing and Publication

    The submitted extended abstracts will be reviewed by the Program Committee.

    Informal proceedings (of abstracts accepted for presentation) will be available from the conference web page. The authors are free to submit their abstracts for publication in other forums.

    The authors of extended abstracts accepted for presentation at the conference will be invited to submit their full papers for publication in formal post proceedings of ADG 2027 after the conference. A special issue in Annals of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence (AMAI) was organized for ADG 2021, 2023, 2025, while former post-proceedings for ADG appeared as special issues of Lecture Notes in Artificial Intelligence (LNAI).
